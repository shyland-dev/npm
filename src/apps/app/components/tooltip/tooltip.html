Tooltip

shyTooltip="text" shows a short hint after --shy-delay-tooltip on mouse hover and right away on keyboard focus (not on touch). It complements the element's name, never replaces it: the text is added to aria-describedby only while open. Esc dismisses without moving focus, the pointer can move onto the tip, and a click closes it.
