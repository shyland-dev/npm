Menu

[shyMenu] on a button opens a menu (APG menu button). Enter, Space or click open on the first item, ↑ opens on the last. Arrows wrap, Home/End jump, typing a letter jumps to the matching item. Activating an item closes the menu; Esc and Tab close it too. Disabled items stay focusable (aria-disabled) but do nothing.
