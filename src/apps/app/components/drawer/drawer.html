Drawer

DrawerService.open() slides a modal panel in from start, end, top or bottom (logical sides: start follows the text direction). It behaves like the dialog (inert page, focus trap, Esc) and, unlike it, a click on the backdrop closes it. The body scrolls inside the panel.

Drag to close

dragToClose lets touch and pen users drag the header (or the handle, with showHandle, on bottom) towards the edge to close; the X button, Esc and the backdrop remain the alternatives without dragging. The body keeps scrolling.
