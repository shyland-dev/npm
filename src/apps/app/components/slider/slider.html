Slider

Single-value slider on the native <input type="range">: ← → ↑ ↓ change by step, Page Up/Page Down by more, Home/End go to the ends. The role and aria-valuenow/min/max come from the browser. For a range, use two sliders.

Default with [(value)]

value: {{ volume() }}

In a form field, with min, max, step and displayWith

value: {{ price.value }}
