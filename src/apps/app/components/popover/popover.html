Popover

[shyPopover] opens a non-modal panel anchored to the element (aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded, aria-controls). Focus goes to the first focusable inside; Esc, a click outside or the trigger again close it and return the focus. Tabbing out of the panel closes it and continues from the trigger. It flips and shifts to stay in the viewport.
