Snackbar

SnackbarService.show(message, options) shows a toast with no element in the template: the container is created on the first call, in the toast layer. Toasts queue (3 at a time), pause while hovered or focused (and when the tab is hidden), and danger uses role="alert"; the rest role="status". An action is hidden while a modal is open.
