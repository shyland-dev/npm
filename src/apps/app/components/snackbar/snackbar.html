Snackbar

SnackbarService.show(message, options) shows a toast with no element in the template: the container is created on the first call, in the toast layer. Toasts queue (3 at a time), pause while hovered or focused (and when the tab is hidden), and danger uses role="alert"; the rest role="status". An action is hidden while a modal is open.

Swipe to dismiss, undo and progress

On touch and pen, drag a toast sideways to dismiss it (past 40% of its width, or a quick flick); the X button is the alternative without a gesture. undo() resolves 'undo' or 'commit'; promise() turns one toast from loading into success or error; the same id updates the open toast. provideShySnackbar({{ '{' }} focusHotkey: 'Alt+T' {{ '}' }}) adds a keyboard shortcut that focuses the latest toast.
