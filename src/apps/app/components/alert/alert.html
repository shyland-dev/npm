Alert

<shy-alert> pairs an icon (different per intent), a title and a message, so it never informs by color alone. It is role="status" by default; urgent makes it role="alert" only for warning and danger. Use it for content that appears after load, and keep urgent alerts rare.

Intents

@for (intent of intents; track intent) { A short message that explains what happened and what to do next. }
