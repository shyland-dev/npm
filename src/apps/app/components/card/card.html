Card

<shy-card> has header and footer slots around the body, and elevation 0 to 3 (--shy-shadow-*). For a clickable card use a native element, <a shyCard href> or <button shyCard>: focus ring, hover (pointer devices only) and pressed state come with it. An interactive card must not contain other controls.
