App shell

<shy-app-shell> lays out the page with projected slots: slot="header", slot="sidebar", the default content (main) and slot="footer". It brings the landmarks (banner, navigation, main, contentinfo) and a "skip to content" link (press Tab once inside the box). In portrait orientation the sidebar becomes a Drawer opened from a menu button. No router dependency: links are yours.

Default (sidebarMode="auto")

Inline sidebar from md up; narrow the window to see it turn into the Drawer.
