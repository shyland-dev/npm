Known limit (WCAG 1.4.4)

At 200% browser zoom the viewport in CSS px halves, so the vmin part of ru shrinks too: text grows about 1.6× (FHD) to 1.8× (phone), not 2×. The rem part follows the user's font setting. Documented in accessibility.md.
