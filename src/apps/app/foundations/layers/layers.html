Layers

Named z-index scale for elements outside the top layer, in steps of 100 so z(modal, 1) fits between two layers. Elements in the top layer (<dialog>.showModal(), [popover]) ignore z-index among themselves and follow the opening order; OverlayService promotes popovers, tooltips and toasts above a newer dialog.

Overlay order

Each button opens a panel through OverlayService.open(). Open them in different orders: a popover or toast opened before a modal is promoted above it, and Esc closes only the topmost one. With a modal open the rest of the page is inert, so close it from its own button or with Esc.
