Layers

Named z-index scale for elements outside the top layer, in steps of 100 so z(modal, 1) fits between two layers. Elements in the top layer (<dialog>.showModal(), [popover]) ignore z-index among themselves and follow the opening order; the overlay service (phase F4) promotes tooltips and toasts above newer dialogs.
